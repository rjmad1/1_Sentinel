# ADR-002: Domain-Driven Design as Primary Architecture Style

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The platform must support modular extensions (Forecasting, Remediation, AI Reasoning). A single, massive database schema would lead to high coupling.

## Decision
Separate the platform into 10 Bounded Contexts. Each context maintains its own data schemas, domain actions, and event mappings. Data sharing is limited to event broadcasts.

## Consequences
- Modular development.
- Higher initial setup costs due to service separation.
