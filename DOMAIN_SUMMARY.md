# Sentinel (EIIP) Domain Summary

## Key Concepts
- **Tenant & Site**: Boundaries of organization partition.
- **Machine & Asset**: Scanned endpoints and their physical or software properties.
- **Finding & Risk**: Issues found via assessment rules and their calculated business impacts.
- **Topology Graph**: Interconnected entity network (Machines, Processes, Services) linked by dependency relationships (`HOSTS`, `RUNS`, `DEPENDS_ON`, `CONNECTS_TO`).
- **Remediation & Validation**: Multi-step workflows to correct and verify environment health.
