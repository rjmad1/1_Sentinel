# ADR-003: Property Graph as a First-Class Capability

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Infrastructure topologies are highly interconnected networks of relationships. Relational databases struggle with deep path traversals.

## Decision
Adopt JanusGraph (Apache TinkerPop Gremlin) as the principal storage engine for representing infrastructure dependency paths.

## Consequences
- Fast dependency tracing and risk propagation analysis.
- Enables Graph RAG integration with LlamaIndex for AI agents.
